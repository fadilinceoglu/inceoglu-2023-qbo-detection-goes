## NOAA GOES magnetometer data

The calculation uses NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)
holdings:

- [GOES 1–15 space-weather instruments](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/goes-1-15/space-weather-instruments),
  including one-minute magnetometer observations used from 1977-08-01 through
  2020-03-02; and
- the [GOES-I through GOES-P high-resolution Level-2 magnetometer archive](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ncei.swx%3Amagn-l2-goes-i-p)
  and [GOES-R magnetometer products](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/goes-r-magnetometer),
  used from 1995-07-01 through 2023-07-03.

The complete minute-resolution GOES source files are downloaded from NCEI and are
not tracked in this repository. The compact three-month values under
`data/processed/quarterly/` are included so that Figures 2–6 can be regenerated
without the full source archive. These released inputs are derived from the NCEI
observations and retain their NOAA NCEI attribution.

Users are responsible for consulting the metadata and terms supplied with the
selected NCEI products. Cite NOAA NCEI and identify the relevant GOES missions and
products in work that uses these observations.

## Coordinate transformation implementation

The focused EPN-to-ECI equations in `src/qbo_detection/coordinates.py` follow the GOES-R
Magnetometer Alternate Coordinate Systems algorithm used by the original
calculation, with the SWPC `magcor` and Loto'aniu `goesMag` implementation
heritage identified in the source documentation. This repository contains a
NumPy reimplementation of the required equations; it does not redistribute
those external source trees. The subsequent ECI-to-GSM conversion and orbital
propagation use SpacePy and SGP4, respectively, under the terms of those
packages.

## Space-Track orbital elements

Two-line element sets used for the EPN-to-GSM conversion are obtained through
[Space-Track](https://www.space-track.org/documentation). Space-Track requires an
account and acceptance of its user agreement. Orbital-element responses and
credentials are not tracked in this repository. Credentials are read from
`SPACETRACK_IDENTITY` and `SPACETRACK_PASSWORD` at run time.

Users must follow Space-Track's current access, citation, rate-limit, and
redistribution requirements.
